## Task

1. Sketch **ethane** in Ligand and examine the 3D model.
2. Rotate the view so you look straight down the C-C bond. The front carbon appears as the center of the projection, and the back carbon sits behind it.
3. Observe the six hydrogens. Are the front and back hydrogens lined up, or offset from each other?

## Solution

The front hydrogens and back hydrogens are offset by **60°**, so the six hydrogens appear evenly spaced around the center. The molecule is in a **staggered** conformation, which is its ground state.
