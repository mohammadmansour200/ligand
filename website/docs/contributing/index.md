# Contributing to Ligand

Ligand is an open-source, cross-platform application for sketching, 3D visualizing, and analyzing organic chemistry structure. Whether you are a cheminformatics researcher, an Android/desktop Kotlin engineer, a UI/UX designer, or a technical writer, your contributions make computational chemistry accessible and open to students, educators, and scientists around the globe.

## Ways to Contribute

There are many valuable ways to contribute to Ligand:

- Suggest new activities for utilizing Ligand in education
- Report bugs you encounter through [Github Tracker](https://github.com/mohammadmansour200/ligand/issues)
- Suggest new features or enhancements through [Github Tracker](https://github.com/mohammadmansour200/ligand/issues)
- Support Ligand development financially through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/mohammadmansour200) or [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/ligand)
